Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Welcome to FictionQuiz. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected and used when you visit and interact with the FictionQuiz website.

1. Information We Collect

FictionQuiz does not require visitors to create an account or provide sensitive personal information to take quizzes.

Some quizzes may ask you to enter a name before starting. This name is used to display your quiz result during your current quiz experience. We do not ask for passwords, payment information or other sensitive personal information through the quiz.

FictionQuiz uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the website. Google Analytics may collect information such as pages viewed, approximate usage information, device information and interactions with the website.

This information helps us understand website traffic and improve the content and user experience of FictionQuiz.

4. Quiz Results and Sharing

FictionQuiz may provide features that allow visitors to share quiz results or challenge friends. When you choose to share a result, the information displayed in the shared result may be included in the link or message you choose to share.

You are responsible for deciding what information you choose to share with others.

5. Third-Party Services

FictionQuiz may use third-party services such as Google Analytics and, in the future, advertising services such as Google AdSense. These services may collect or process information according to their own privacy policies.

6. Children's Privacy

FictionQuiz is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly request sensitive personal information from children.

7. External Links

FictionQuiz may contain links to external websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact FictionQuiz through the contact information provided on the website.
